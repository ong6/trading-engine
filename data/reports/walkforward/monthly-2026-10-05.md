# Walk-forward at monthly granularity — 2026-10-05

Generated by `farm/walkforward/monthly.py`. Same replays as the fold-level report, re-read one month at a time. Nothing here changes a fold-level verdict; it is the second reading the evaluation asked for.

## Rule (mechanical exploratory triage)

* Unit: paired monthly excess return, book − control, over validate months only. The control comes from the versioned declaration stored in each result; legacy results without one receive `NO-CONTROL`.
* Fold 10 is **dropped** for every book registered before 2026-08-01: it is the data those rules were written against. Registrations on or after 2026-08-01 keep it (flagged in `Folds dropped`).
* CIs: stationary block bootstrap (Politis-Romano), geometric blocks with mean 4 months, 10,000 draws, seed 20260820, 90% two-sided, on the mean and on the median.
* NW t: Newey-West t on the mean, Bartlett kernel, 3 lags.
* DSR: farm.stats.deflated_sharpe on the pooled months, n_trials = number of books in the table (fallback variance); bar 0.95. Context only.
* **BEATS** = median CI entirely > 0 AND NW t ≥ +1.645. **TRAILS** = median CI entirely < 0 AND NW t ≤ −1.645. Otherwise **INDISTINGUISHABLE**; fewer than 24 months = **NO-DATA**. Median carries the verdict (the fold PASS keyed on a mean one fold could hijack); the NW t on the mean is required corroboration.
