## Evidence limits and live gate

- Single-name history uses today's survivors and liquidity flags. Coverage is
  only about 42% of operating companies in 2014 and 11.4% in 1996. Therefore
  single-name absolute backtest returns are not production acceptance evidence.
- The paper league has only 36 sessions. No live excess-return estimate is
  statistically compelling.
- The simulator omits commissions/fees, taxes, explicit ETF expense drag, order
  book depth, partial fills, and market impact beyond its liquidity-tier model.
- Discretionary stops are metadata, not executable protective orders. Strategy
  kill criteria are review prompts, not hard kill switches. Future corporate-
  action warnings and stale marks still require human resolution.
- There is no broker integration. Any live deployment requires a separately
  reviewed execution layer, reconciliation, hard pre-trade limits, alerts, and
  a tested kill switch.

## Post-review v4 capital evidence — 2026-09-07

The production fill model is now `v4`, with named baseline and doubled-cost
profiles and persisted starting capital. Complete five-year replays were run at
$10k, $39k, $100k, $250k, $1m and $10m for the two ETF candidates, SPY, and a
stock-basket contrast. All 48 cells share one source hash and the repaired APH

- SPY had one fill and zero capacity rejects through $10m; doubling costs moved
  CAGR only from 12.85% to 12.84%.
- Sector momentum and dual momentum had zero capacity rejects through the
  sampled $1m point, but rejected orders at $10m. At $39k, doubled friction
  reduced CAGR by 0.71 and 0.37 percentage points respectively.
- `xs_momentum_12_1` failed the zero-rejection gate at the smallest tested
  balance: 4 capacity rejects at $10k, 25 at $39k, 315 at $1m and 1,734 at
  $10m. At $39k, doubled friction reduced CAGR from 24.91% to 23.06%.

These measurements strengthen the WATCH/paper-only decision. ETF evidence is
cleaner and materially more scalable, but neither ETF strategy has established
statistical excess over SPY. The stock basket's attractive absolute history is
both survivor-biased and capacity-constrained, so it is not production
acceptance evidence. Full tables and provenance are under
`data/reports/capital-sensitivity/`.

## Research basis

The design is consistent with the broad time-series-momentum literature, but
that literature mainly studies volatility-scaled long/short futures and does
not establish this long-only ETF/cash implementation. Relevant counterevidence
also finds weak asset-by-asset TSMOM after dependence corrections. See
Moskowitz–Ooi–Pedersen (2012), doi:10.1016/j.jfineco.2011.11.003;
Kim–Tse–Wald (2016), doi:10.1016/j.finmar.2016.05.003; and
Huang–Li–Wang–Zhou (2020), doi:10.1016/j.jfineco.2019.08.004. Backtest-selection
discipline follows White (2000), doi:10.1111/1468-0262.00152, and the Deflated
Sharpe Ratio, Bailey & López de Prado (2014). Paper/live limitations are also
consistent with Alpaca's official paper-trading warning:
https://docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/paper-trading.
